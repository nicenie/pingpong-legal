Privacy Policy

Ping Pong Scoreboard · Effective 23 October 2026

This app does not connect to the internet and does not collect any data. No account, no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs. The matches you record stay on your own phone.

1. What we collect

Nothing. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Accounts, names, phone numbers or email addresses
  • Location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data
  • Device identifiers such as IDFA or IDFV
  • Usage behaviour, crash logs or any analytics

2. Where your data lives

Match records, the player list and app settings are stored in the app's local sandbox on your device. We have no server, so no copy of this data exists anywhere else. Deleting the app deletes this data with it.

The share-card feature renders an image on your own device and hands it to the iOS share sheet. The image never reaches a server, so we cannot see what you share or who you share it with.

This app contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK and no other third-party libraries.

5. Tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites, so the app never asks for tracking permission.

We do not know who our users are and we collect no data from anyone, including children.

7. Your rights

Your data exists only on your device. You can delete individual records inside the app at any time, or delete everything by removing the app. We cannot export or erase your data for you, because we never receive it.

8. Changes

If this policy changes with an app update, we will revise this page and update the effective date above.

Никакие. В частности, мы не собираем:
